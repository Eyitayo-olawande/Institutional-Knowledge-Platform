# content

Industry templates and country and regulator packs, kept as data, not code.

Version 1: the insurance template and the Nigerian regulator pack. Template and pack items are copied into a company's database at onboarding, each carrying a reference to its source item and version. New versions arrive as proposed updates that the company accepts, edits or declines.

All regulatory content is verified and approved by SUNU Compliance before use.
